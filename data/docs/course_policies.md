# Course policies — Building LLM Applications with LangChain + Agents

The course meets Tuesdays and Thursdays from 6:00 PM to 9:00 PM from November 3 to November 19, 2026, for six sessions and eighteen hours in total. Office hours are Wednesdays 6:00–8:00 PM or Saturdays 10:00 AM–12:00 PM.

Grading: labs are 60 percent, homework is 15 percent, and the capstone demo plus repository is 25 percent. The capstone rubric awards 8 points for a working end-to-end demo, 6 points for using at least three course concepts, 5 points for an evaluation with at least ten cases, 4 points for a README with an architecture diagram, and 2 points for code quality.

Capstone proposals are due Thursday, November 12 and are approved by Saturday, November 14 office hours. The repository link is due Saturday, November 21 at noon. Teams may have one to three members. Demos are six minutes each on Thursday, November 19.

AI coding assistants are allowed and encouraged; students must be able to explain every line they submit. Labs are designed to cost under one dollar per session on small models; Ollama can be used locally for zero cost.

The instructor is Lance Muwayi. The program is Tech Launchpad at City Colleges of Chicago, Kennedy-King College.
